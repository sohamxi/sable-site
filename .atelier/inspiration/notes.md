## Theft declaration (structure, rhythm, density, motion only — no palettes, wordmarks, imagery, or copy)

- From **Teenage Engineering**: model-number typography as ornament, black-monolith product cards, and the dense→sparse density oscillation.
- From **Nothing**: the frosted-glass product card floating over full-bleed photography, and one-sentence-per-product card grammar for the range.
- From **Bang & Olufsen**: hero grammar — atmospheric human-in-environment photography, product name bottom-left, ghost CTA.
- From **Daylight**: headline set inside the photograph + UI as floating corner cards; the lesson that canvas temperature can argue the product's thesis.
- From **Even Realities** (via scout): flagship-plus-companion told as one narrative spine.

Explicitly NOT taken: Nothing's dot-matrix face and parenthetical naming (brand DNA), TE's red/orange Swedish-workshop identity, B&O heritage framing, Daylight's amber palette, any photography or copy.

## Category default (scout synthesis)

Dark cinematic canvas + full-bleed renders + monumental type is still run by B&O/Apple, but the 2025-26 craft ceiling has moved to light/warm utilitarian-industrial and editorial registers (Nothing, TE, Daylight, Even Realities, Oura). Dark-cinematic-with-floating-render reads late-2023 template unless render quality is genuinely Apple-tier. Decision for SABLE: join-and-out-execute, or break within the dark constraint the user locked.
