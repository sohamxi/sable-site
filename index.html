01FLAGSHIP

Five decisions, one earphone.

Each one cost something. Each one is a number you can check against the plot in your box.

01 · ATTENUATION
42 dB
PARTS 04, 06 · MIC ARRAY, MESH GRILLE

Quiet you can verify

Six microphones close the loop every 19 microseconds. Worn attenuation at 1 kHz reaches 42 dB, passive seal plus active loop. The rating comes from production units pulled at random. A golden sample would flatter it, and we do not keep one.

02 · DRIVER
11.2 mm
PART 05 · TITANIUM-COATED DIAPHRAGM, COPPER COIL

Sub-40 Hz, sealed

Reaching below 40 Hz usually costs a bass vent, and a vent costs the seal. The 11.2 mm titanium-coated driver does it with displacement instead. Distortion holds under 0.08% at 94 dB SPL.

03 · FIT
6.1 g
PARTS 01, 02 · TOUCH PLATE, SHELL HALF

Weight the ear ignores

Each bud weighs 6.1 grams, its mass set 1.2 mm forward of the canal axis so the seal carries the load. The outer face is a flat plate turned with concentric grooves, 0.2 mm apart. Under a thumb it reads like the face of a record. You find it without looking.

04 · POWER
32 h
PART 03 · 68 mAh CELL, STEEL CAN

Two days between docks

Eight hours sealed and cancelling, rated at 75 dB SPL rather than at a whisper. The case holds 24 more. Ten minutes on the dock returns two hours of playback.

05 · LINK
19 ms
PART 04 · RADIO BOARD, BLUETOOTH 5.4

Close to the wire

Bluetooth 5.4 with LE Audio and LC3. Game mode holds latency at 19 ms. Two hosts stay connected at once and switch in under 80 ms.

02RANGE

Four instruments. One discipline.

Built to one tolerance on one rig. SB-01, SB-02 and SW-01 ship with calibration certificates.

The plot in the box matches the serial on the shell. Keep it. It is the unit's only birth record.
